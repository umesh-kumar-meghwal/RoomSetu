Rules and Guidelines for Students & Property Owners on RoomSetu

1. Platform Nature

RoomSetu is an educational discovery portal connecting students and property owners. We are not brokers, real estate agents, or property caretakers. Rental contracts and security deposit transfers are strictly agreements between the student and the landlord.

2. Student Tenancy Conduct

Students agree to abide by the declared house rules (curfew hours, visitor permissions, quiet hours, cooking guidelines). Any unlawful activity or harassment reported by the owner will lead to immediate account termination.

3. Landlord Obligations & Honest Disclosures

Owners must provide authentic photographs, accurate GPS pins, and truthful electricity sub-meter policies. Demanding unaccounted extra charges or misrepresenting room occupancy will result in instant listing suspension.
