Frequently Asked Questions

Sabhi aam sawaalon ke jawab jo students aur parents poochte hain

Kya RoomSetu par room dhundne ke koi charges hain? ↓

Nahi! RoomSetu students ke liye 100% free platform hai. Aap bina kisi brokerage ya platform fee ke direct property owner se contact kar sakte hain.

Mujhe kaise pata chalega ki owner genuine hai? ↓

RoomSetu par koi bhi landlord tab tak room publish nahi kar sakta jab tak admin uske Bijli Bill ya Property Tax Receipt ki jaanch karke KYC approve na kar de. Sath hi real student reviews bhi available hote hain.

"Nearby Rooms Radar" kaise kaam karta hai? ↓

Jab aap "Nearby Rooms" page par jakar 'Use My Current Location' par click karte hain, toh browser GPS ke zariye aapke 1km, 3km ya 5km ke dayre mein maujood sabhi vacant rooms ko Leaflet map par show kar deta hai.

Agar koi landlord galat details ya photo daalta hai toh? ↓

Har property par "Report Listing" ka option hota hai. Student ki complaint aate hi admin team 24 ghante ke andar listing ko suspend aur landlord ka account terminate kar deti hai.
